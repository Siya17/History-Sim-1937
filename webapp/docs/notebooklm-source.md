## Exact mechanics to preserve

- Rules version: Classroom Rules v2.
- Economy development spends two diplomatic influence and one action. Gain two economy with 70% probability; otherwise gain one.
- Teams share two actions per round across devices.
- Sending trade/alliance proposals spends no actions or resources, but a proposing team must still have an action available.
- Acceptance charges both sides, after checking their current resources and remaining actions.
- Trade: one action and one diplomatic influence per country; each gains one economy. One accepted trade per country pair per round.
- Practice uses prepared results and never changes the real game.
- Every participating country earns one victory point per round. Independent countries receive their listed country bonuses as well.
- The teacher controls rounds. The game does not have sequential player turns.
